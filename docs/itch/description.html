🎮 What is Loot Silo?

Loot Silo is a game asset manager for game devs: a desktop asset library organizer and sprite / spritesheet viewer for everything you have bought, bundled or downloaded — pixel art, tilesets, characters, UI, VFX, 3D models, PBR materials, sound effects, music and fonts.

Import your packs once, then find any sprite, sound, model or texture in seconds, watch animations play, and copy the credits into your game.

✨ Features

🔍 Find anything in seconds

  • Search as you type across pack titles, authors, tags and every file name — "barr" finds barrels, HumanSoldier counts as "human" and "soldier".
  • Filter by type (character, environment, UI, icons, VFX, SFX, music, 3D, material, font), author, tag or source.
  • ⭐ Star your favourites; sort by best match, name, author, size, file count, tags or type.
  • Packs, Expanded and Files views, a thumbnail slider from tiny to huge, and pack covers that play their animations.

🏃 Sprite sheets that just play

  • Sprite sheets, strips, grids, GIFs and animated WebPs are detected and played automatically, at the right frame size — all at once or on hover.
  • Guess wrong? Fix frame size, frame count, row and FPS in the Edit dialog, or mark it "Not an animation".
  • Make animation… turns any image into a sheet; combine single frame files into one strip.
  • Copy as GIF — to the clipboard or as a file — for your devlog, Discord or game jam page.

🖼️ Pixel-perfect image viewer

  • Images open at 1:1, actual pixels, so pixel art stays crisp. Switch to Fit, zoom with the wheel, drag to pan.
  • Focus mode steps through cards with the arrow keys.
  • Right-click any file: copy image, copy file, copy path, open with… your editor of choice.

🧊 3D models

  • A Models tab with rendered cards: GLB, glTF and OBJ are drawn in their own colours and textures — hover a card to turn the model.
  • One card per model, whatever formats it ships in (GLB + OBJ + FBX = one card with a chip per format).
  • Model viewer: orbit, zoom and pan, wireframe, flat colours, turntable and floor grid, with triangles, vertices, size, textures and materials at a glance.

🎨 Textures and PBR materials

  • Color, Normal, Roughness, Metalness, AO… maps become one card per material instead of flooding your image list.
  • Grouped by name or by a MaterialX .mtlx file; spots files for Godot, USD, Blender, Substance and Unity.
  • Material viewer on a lit sphere or plane, tiled, or one map at a time — drag to move the light.

🔊 Sound effects browser

  • Play SFX and music right in the pack — Play all, Loop, seek — without opening an audio editor.
  • Waveforms on every sound, so a short click and a long whoosh look different before you press play.
  • A sound saved as WAV, OGG and MP3 is one card with a chip per format; pick the format you prefer.
  • Sort by name, length, file size, sample rate, channels, bitrate or bit depth.

📦 Import from anywhere

  • Drag and drop folders or archives (zip, tar, 7z, rar).
  • Loot Silo finds the packs inside, guesses author and type, and shows a review before anything is copied.
  • Every copied file is checked against its SHA-1. Your originals are never touched.
  • Big collections become bundles that hold sub-packs.

📜 Credits and licenses

  • Keep the license and attribution for every author.
  • Mark which packs a game uses and get its credits ready to paste — or save them as CREDITS.md / CREDITS.txt.
  • Read license and readme files inside the app, Markdown rendered.

🗂️ Tabs, resume and themes

  • Open packs in tabs to compare them (middle-click, Ctrl+Tab, drag to reorder).
  • Loot Silo reopens where you left off — tabs, filters, sort, view, even folded folders.
  • Light or dark, with a choice of colour themes.
  • Press F1 for the full guide and every keyboard shortcut.

💚 Why Loot Silo

  • Plain files. Your library is one folder arranged as Author / Pack, with small text files you can edit by hand. Stop using Loot Silo and your library is still a tidy folder.
  • No cloud, no account. Everything stays on your disk. The only network calls are the itch.io ones you turn on.
  • Made for game dev. It knows what a sprite sheet, a tileset, a PBR material and a credits screen are.

📁 Supported formats

  • Images: PNG, GIF, JPG, WebP, BMP, SVG, TGA (Aseprite and PSD listed)
  • Audio: OGG, WAV, MP3, FLAC, M4A, AIFF, Opus
  • 3D: GLB, glTF, OBJ drawn; FBX, DAE, STL, Blend, 3DS, VOX listed
  • Materials: PBR maps, MaterialX, Godot .tres, USD, Blender, Substance, Unity
  • Fonts: TTF, OTF, WOFF, FNT
  • Archives: zip, tar, 7z, rar
